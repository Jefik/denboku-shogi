ShogiWestern.otf, ShogiCursive.otf, ShogiPrintoutKanji2.otf and the piece colors (Colored pieces)

Traced with tools/build_trace_font.py from the "uniform_western" SVG pieces of
"Western Shogi Printout-Set" by peanatsu, https://github.com/peanatsu/shogi_printout
(release v1.0, svgsrc_and_xboard.tar.gz); the piece bodies were left out.

The chess images in that set come from Wikimedia Commons, under the same license:
- king, rook, bishop, knight: Colin M.L. Burnett
- pawn: NikNaks
- lance: Orror/Zigeuner
- promoted rook, bishop, silver, knight and lance: Hari Seldon

ShogiCursive.otf: the cursive promoted pieces of the "2kanji" xboard tiles (Hari Seldon).
ShogiPrintoutKanji2.otf: the unpromoted pieces of the "2kanji" xboard tiles; their kanji are
AR PL UKai CN glyphs, (c) 1999 Arphic Technology Co., Ltd., so this font is also under the
Arphic Public License (ARPHICPL.TXT).

Changes (2026-11-08 and 2026-11-09): the symbols were traced into fonts, one glyph per piece
at its 1-kanji code point (--printout 1 and --xboard 1); the piece shape is the game's own.
