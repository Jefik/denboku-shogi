# The tutorial's lessons for Minishogi (5×5; format: "Lessons" in docs/CODE.md).

chapter Basics

lesson minishogi Minishōgi
about Shōgi on 5×5
step A small board
text Minishōgi is shōgi on a 5×5 board, without knights and lances: a king, a gold, a silver, a bishop, a rook and a pawn each. Pieces move, capture and drop as in shōgi. Collect the stars with the rook.
position 5/5/5/5/4R b - 1
goal stars 1b 4b
limit 2
solution 1e1b 1b4b
step One rank to promote
text The promotion zone is only the last rank. Move the pawn there; it must promote.
position 5/2P2/5/5/5 b - 1
goal move 3b3a+
step Rook and bishop
text The rook and the bishop promote too, into dragon and horse, as in shōgi. Move the bishop into the last rank and promote it.
position 5/5/4B/5/5 b - 1
goal move 1c3a+
step Mate
text Mate the king with the gold, guarded by the pawn.
position 2k2/5/2P2/5/5 b G 1
goal mate
solution G*3b
