# The tutorial's lessons for Goro Goro (5×6; format: "Lessons" in docs/CODE.md).

chapter Basics

lesson goro Goro Goro
about The beginners' shōgi on 5×6
step A small board
text Goro Goro is the shōgi the Japan Shōgi Association teaches children with: a 5×6 board with a king, two golds, two silvers and three pawns each. They move as in shōgi.
text Collect the stars with the silver.
position 5/5/5/5/2S2/5 b - 1
goal stars 3d 2c 3b
limit 3
solution 3e3d 3d2c 2c3b
step Promotion
text Here the promotion zone is the last two ranks. Move the pawn into it and promote it: it then moves like a gold.
position 5/5/2P2/5/5/5 b - 1
goal move 3c3b+
step Mate
text Drops, 二歩 and 打ち歩詰め work as in shōgi. Mate the king with the gold, guarded by the pawn.
position 2k2/5/2P2/5/5/5 b G 1
goal mate
solution G*3b
