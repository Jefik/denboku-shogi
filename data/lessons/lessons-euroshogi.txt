# The tutorial's lessons for Euroshogi (8×8; format: "Lessons" in docs/CODE.md).

chapter Basics

lesson euroshogi Euroshōgi
about Shōgi on 8×8, with a knight that steps aside
step The knight
text Euroshōgi is shōgi on an 8×8 board. Its knight jumps as in shōgi, two squares forward and one to the side, and it can also step one square sideways. Collect the stars with it.
position 8/8/8/8/8/8/3N4/8 b - 1
goal stars 4g 3e
limit 2
solution 5g4g 4g3e
step Three ranks, no choice
text The promotion zone is the last three ranks, and a piece that moves into, within or out of it always promotes. Move the pawn into the zone.
position 8/8/8/4P3/8/8/8/8 b - 1
goal move 4d4c+
step Mate with a knight
text A knight can be dropped anywhere, since it can always step sideways. Mate the king with a knight drop: the bishop covers its way out.
position 6gk/7p/8/4B3/8/8/8/8 b N 1
goal mate
solution N*2c
