# The tutorial's lessons for Kyotoshogi (5×5; format: "Lessons" in docs/CODE.md). Every move of a
# piece turns it over, so every move is written with +.

chapter Basics

lesson kyoto Kyotoshōgi
about Every move turns the piece over
step Silver and bishop
text In Kyotoshōgi every piece but the king turns over each time it moves: silver and bishop, gold and knight, pawn and rook, lance and tokin share a piece. Move the silver to the star: it lands as a bishop. Then move the bishop to the next star.
position 5/5/2S2/5/5 b - 1
goal stars 3b 5d
limit 2
solution 3c3b+ 3b5d+
step Pawn and rook
text The pawn steps forward and becomes a rook; the rook moves and becomes a pawn again. Collect the stars.
position 5/5/5/2P2/5 b - 1
goal stars 3c 1c
limit 2
solution 3d3c+ 3c1c+
step Gold and knight
text The gold moves and becomes a knight; the knight jumps two squares forward and one to the side, and becomes a gold. Collect the stars.
position 5/5/5/2G2/5 b - 1
goal stars 3c 2a
limit 2
solution 3d3c+ 3c2a+
step Tokin and lance
text The tokin moves like a gold and becomes a lance; the lance slides forward and becomes a tokin. Collect the stars.
position 5/5/5/5/2+L2 b - 1
goal stars 3d 3a
limit 2
solution 3e3d+ 3d3a+
